Rating Guide

Understanding Food Hygiene Ratings

Food hygiene ratings are given by local authorities in the UK after inspecting food businesses. Ratings range from 0 (urgent improvement necessary) to 5 (very good). All data is sourced from the Food Standards Agency and Bristol City Council's open data platform.

Jump to a rating

  2. 4 Good
  3. 3 Generally Satisfactory
  4. 2 Improvement Necessary

  6. 0 Urgent Improvement Required

  1. Very Good

    5

    Very Good

    Very good hygiene standards. Food handling, cleanliness, and record-keeping all meet or exceed requirements.

    What this means: This business has passed all aspects of the food hygiene inspection to the highest standard. You can be confident in the cleanliness and safety of food prepared here.

  2. Good

    4

    Good

    Good hygiene standards overall. Minor issues may exist but the business is performing well.

    What this means: The business is performing well. Any issues noted during the inspection are minor and the business is managing its hygiene responsibly.

  3. Generally Satisfactory

    3

    Generally Satisfactory

    Hygiene standards are acceptable but some areas need improvement. Basic requirements are met.

    What this means: The business meets minimum legal standards but there is room for improvement. Basic food safety requirements are being met.

  4. Improvement Necessary

    2

    Improvement Necessary

    Notable hygiene issues found. The business must take action to improve its standards.

    What this means: There are notable hygiene concerns. The business has been informed of what needs to be corrected. You may wish to consider this before visiting.

  5. Major Improvement Necessary

    1

    Major Improvement Necessary

    Poor hygiene standards. Significant changes are required to reach acceptable levels.

    What this means: There are serious hygiene concerns at this establishment. You are advised to exercise caution and may wish to avoid visiting until an improved rating is issued.

  6. Urgent Improvement Required

    0

    Urgent Improvement Required

    Serious concerns found. Urgent action is required and enforcement action may be taken.

    What this means: This business has very poor hygiene standards. Formal enforcement action may be underway. It is strongly recommended you avoid this establishment.

Special Statuses

  • Awaiting Inspection

    The business is newly registered and has not yet been inspected by the local authority. No rating is available. This does not mean the business is unsafe — it simply has not been inspected yet.

  • New Rating Pending

    A recent inspection has taken place. The displayed rating may not reflect the latest findings and could change. Check back regularly for the updated score.

  • Exempt

    This business is exempt from the food hygiene rating scheme. Certain premises — such as some low-risk or temporary establishments — are not required to be rated.
