What Does This Rating Mean?

Food hygiene ratings are given to food businesses following an inspection by local food safety officers. Ratings range from 0 (urgent improvement necessary) to 5 (very good).

  1. 5

    Very Good Very good hygiene standards. Food handling, cleanliness, and record-keeping all meet or exceed requirements.

  2. 4

    Good Good hygiene standards overall. Minor issues may exist but the business is performing well.

  3. 3

    Generally Satisfactory Hygiene standards are acceptable but some areas need improvement. Basic requirements are met.

  4. 2

    Improvement Necessary Notable hygiene issues found. The business must take action to improve its standards.

  5. 1

    Major Improvement Necessary Poor hygiene standards. Significant changes are required to reach acceptable levels.

  6. 0

    Urgent Improvement Required Serious concerns found. Urgent action is required and enforcement action may be taken.
