What Does This Rating Mean?

Ratings are awarded following an inspection by local food safety officers and range from 0 (urgent improvement required) to 5 (very good).

  • 5

    Very Good All aspects of food hygiene meet or exceed requirements.

  • 4

    Good Good hygiene overall with only minor issues.

  • 3

    Generally Satisfactory Basic requirements met, some areas need attention.

  • 2

    Improvement Necessary Notable issues found. Action is required.

  • 1

    Major Improvement Necessary Poor standards. Significant changes required.

  • 0

    Urgent Improvement Required Serious concerns. Enforcement may be taken.
